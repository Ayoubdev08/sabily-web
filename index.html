01Habits

Small steps, every day.

Track prayer, Quran reading and dhikr, add habits of your own, and watch your streaks grow — by week, month and year.

02SOS

A guided pause when temptation comes.

Breathing, verses, du'as and what to do right now. Sabily counts the temptations you resisted, not the times you fell. Encouraging, never judgmental.

03Privacy

Your position never leaves your phone.

Prayer times are worked out on the device. No ads, no analytics trackers — and you can delete your account and all your data from inside the app.

04Prayer

Accurate times, even offline.

Calculated from where you are, with reminders you turn on one by one, a Qibla compass, and a few minutes of adjustment per prayer to match your mosque.

05Quran

The Quran, page by page.

The full Uthmani text following the 604-page Madani pagination, or verse by verse with translation. Bookmarks, reading goals and Khatma plans keep you consistent.

06Quiz

Learn while you play.

600+ questions across 12 categories — Quran, hadith, prophets, fiqh, history — to grow your knowledge a little every day.

07Hifz

Memorise, and keep it.

Follow your memorisation surah by surah, with spaced repetition that brings each one back at the right time.
