What's inside

Prayer times, on your phone

Times are calculated on your device from your position: no server needed, and they keep working offline. A Qibla compass, and a few minutes of adjustment per prayer to match your mosque.

The Quran, page by page

Read page by page, following the 604-page Madani pagination, or verse by verse with translation. Bookmarks, reading goals and Khatma plans help you stay consistent.

Habits and streaks

Track prayer, Quran reading and dhikr, add habits of your own, and watch your progress by week, month and year.

SOS, for moments of temptation

A guided pause when you need one: breathing, verses, du'as and what to do right now. Encouraging, never judgmental.

Everyday worship tools

Du'as and adhkar, a tasbih counter, the 99 Names of Allah, a hadith collection, a Hifz tracker, fasting and qada trackers, and the Hijri calendar.

Learn and grow together

Islamic quizzes, group challenges, and private circles with friends and family.

Six languages

The interface speaks English, Français, العربية, Español, Deutsch and Italiano, with full right-to-left support for Arabic.

Private by design

  • Your GPS position stays on your phone.
  • No ads and no analytics trackers.
  • Delete your account and your data from inside the app.
