Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Sabily ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account: email address and display name (via Supabase Authentication).
  • Your practice: habits, streaks, Qur’an bookmarks and reading position, memorisation, favourites, challenges and quiz scores — stored so they follow you across devices.
  • Location: used on your device to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. Your coordinates are never sent to our servers.
  • If you enter a city instead, it is sent once to a geocoding service to obtain its coordinates, which are then kept on your device.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the app: prayer times, habit tracking, Qur’an, and the rest.
  • To sync your progress across your devices when you are signed in.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and database (supabase.com/privacy).
  • Geocoding, only if you enter a city by name, to convert it into coordinates.
  • Reciter audio is streamed or downloaded from a public Qur’an audio service.

Prayer times are calculated on your device and require no server.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is kept as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. It cannot be undone.

You can also request deletion by email at the address below.

5. Children’s Privacy

Sabily is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.
